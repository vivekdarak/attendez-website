# Good Times Co: Turning a Stalled Website Project Into a Lead-Generating Digital Presence

Good Times Co needed more than just a website. The founder wanted a digital presence that felt trustworthy, premium, and aligned with the brand's personality. For almost a year, he had been trying to get the website right, but the earlier attempt with a freelancer had resulted in a generic WordPress template that did not reflect the business properly. The design felt disconnected from the brand, the user experience was limited, and even simple changes around layout, colors, navigation, and messaging became difficult.

The founder was particular about the visual identity. The website had to match the logo colors, feel clean and credible, and guide visitors clearly toward the company's services. When he approached Vivek at Attendez, there was a natural concern that this process might again become slow, restrictive, and frustrating, especially when it came to feedback on design, colors, pages, and content.

Instead of starting directly with design, we began with context.

Before building the first version, we prepared a detailed prompt foundation for the project. This included the nature of the business, target customers, services offered, brand tone, logo colors, desired user journey, and the kind of trust the website needed to create. This gave the AI-assisted design process a strong starting point instead of producing a random template.

The first design direction matched the founder's expectations closely, but that was only the beginning. Once the initial version was approved, we moved into fast iteration. The founder shared detailed feedback across content, messaging, sections, pages, typography, colors, and navigation. Each change was quickly implemented, tested, and refined. We adjusted the content to make the services clearer, added new pages where needed, changed the font to better fit the brand, and fine-tuned the color system so the website felt consistent with the logo and overall identity.

A major part of the visual experience was also custom-built. Instead of relying on generic stock images, we generated brand-relevant AI images through careful prompting and iteration over almost two days. This helped the website feel more original and aligned with the company's positioning.

After launch, the next test was performance. We used Google Ads to drive relevant traffic and observe how visitors responded. The result was clear: people trusted the website enough to submit their contact details and enquire about services.

As enquiries started coming in, we noticed a new challenge: fake or low-quality submissions. To protect the founder's time and improve lead quality, we added OTP verification to the contact form. This ensured that every lead submitted through the website was verified before reaching the business.

Today, Good Times Co has a website that not only reflects its brand better, but also supports real business growth. The project moved from a stalled, template-limited website to a custom, trusted, lead-generating platform with verified enquiries and a stronger conversion path.
